# LuciRift

**Developer · Digital Creator · Music Artist**

Founder of [LuciRift LLC](https://lucirift.com), bringing together software development, gaming, music, and digital creativity.

I work across web development, backend systems, server infrastructure, game modding, and immersive digital experiences. My approach is driven by curiosity, continuous learning, and building practical solutions from creative ideas.

---

## About

- **Development:** Web applications, Node.js, APIs, databases, and automation.
- **Infrastructure:** Linux, Docker, self-hosting, and cloud services.
- **Gaming & Modding:** Xbox 360 homebrew, game modifications, and community tools, with roots in the 2017–2021 modding scene.
- **VR & Digital Media:** Unity, VRChat worlds, avatar customization, and digital assets.
- **Music:** Independent Trap, Hip-Hop, and electronic music released under LuciRift.
- **AI:** Integrating AI-assisted tools into development, learning, and creative production.

I view AI as a practical extension of the creative process, helping bridge technical gaps, accelerate learning, and turn ideas into functional projects while maintaining human direction and oversight.

## Current Focus

- Developing and expanding **LuciRift LLC**
- Building and maintaining modern web applications and backend services
- Improving self-hosted infrastructure and automation
- Exploring game modding, homebrew, and VR development
- Producing and releasing original music
- Refining AI-assisted development workflows
